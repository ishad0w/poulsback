# Code of conduct

Poulsback welcomes people contributing code, documentation, tests, research,
hardware observations and questions.

These expectations apply to everyone, including maintainers.

Help keep discussion useful and welcoming to newcomers.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss the change, evidence or technical decision. Criticize ideas without
  personal attacks, insults or speculation about another person's motives.
- Respect different backgrounds, experience levels and communication styles.
  Make room for questions, ask for clarification and accept corrections.
- Credit other people's work accurately and respect their privacy.
- Keep discussion relevant. Give others room to participate and respect a
  request to stop an unwelcome interaction.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, sexual harassment, doxxing, sustained
disruption and retaliation against someone who raises a concern are
unacceptable.

This policy applies in project-managed issues, pull requests, discussions and
other project channels, and when someone represents Poulsback in a public space.

## Reporting and response

Report a concern privately to **Alex** at [alex@kmn.io](mailto:alex@kmn.io).

Include the relevant links or messages, what happened and a way to contact you.

You do not need to debate the concern publicly or confront the person involved.

The maintainer reviews reports in context, seeks clarification when needed and
shares personal details only as needed to handle the report.

Available responses include guidance, a warning, removal of content, a temporary
restriction or a ban.

The response should reflect the behavior, its impact and any repeated
violations.

People affected by a decision may request reconsideration through the same
private contact.

Alex handles moderation for this small project, and replies may take time.

Please respect the privacy of everyone involved while a concern is reviewed.
