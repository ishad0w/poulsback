# Authors and contributors

Poulsback is built through code, documentation, tests, research, bug reports and
hardware observations.

We welcome help in all of these areas and keep contributors' credit with their
work when it is adapted or included in a release.

## Project contributors

This file keeps project credit with source archives and public snapshots, even
when they do not contain the full development history.

It supplements file-level copyright notices and does not replace them or assign
ownership of every file to the people listed here.

## Contribution credit

When your contribution is included, you are welcome to add your preferred public
name or handle and a short description here, or ask a maintainer to add it for
you.

This includes documentation, testing and research help as well as code.

Include collaborators' agreed credit and use `Co-authored-by` trailers where
appropriate.

Share only the names and contact details people agree to publish.

Corrections and missing-credit reports are welcome through
[Support](SUPPORT.md).

Maintainers preserve existing credit when importing, adapting or combining
contributions.

Git authorship and agreed coauthor trailers provide additional detail where
history is available.

[Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md) explains sign-offs, applicable licences and
preservation of original notices.
