# Native 2D status

WIP. The 2D core runs X's fills and copies (EXA in our DDX), Present's copies of
GL frames and Mesa's `pipe->blit` through `SUBMIT_2D`
([execution](../kernel/execution.md)); the
[hardware reference](../hardware.md) owns its device contracts. The native 2D
checklist items keep their own acceptance basis.

## Working and checked

- **Device:** fills and copies with all 16 GX logical operations in ARGB8888,
  XRGB8888 and RGB565 pass selected independent per-bit fixtures on WC/WB
  storage. The wider dimension/edge matrix covers GXcopy and GXxor, including
  width and height eight with no erratum. X11 uses the same core as described
  in [X11 status](x11.md).
- **Device, unaligned destinations:** fills and copies split into a head and
  an aligned body, preserving copy order, clipping and pixels.
- **CPU:** FILL/COPY, clipping, checked overlap/spans and all 16 X11 logical
  operations in ARGB8888, XRGB8888 and RGB565. Independent packet and pixel
  interpreters cover valid inputs, guards and refusal without output changes.

## In progress

- Alpha blending, scaling and general composition are outside this raster
  interface. X11 Render composition beyond fills and copies stays on the CPU;
  the planned 3D-core route has its own [X11 status](x11.md).
- Complete the registered native acceptance selection: combined parameter and
  boundary matrices, multi-client reuse, close/loss behavior, visible motion
  and long stress. These are remaining qualification work; the fill/copy and
  logical-operation implementations already exist.
- Complete the shared engine's safe teardown and suspend/resume lifecycle.
  Device fixtures and CPU tests retain their own scope until the complete
  applicable selection passes.

Details: [2D interface and contracts](../native-2d.md),
[tests](../tests.md#native-2d) and [acceptance](../checklist.md).

## Progress basis

**Approximate implementation progress: 90%.** The scope is the native
fill/copy blitter, its submission path and client integration. General X11
Render composition belongs to [X11 status](x11.md).

The estimate weights the size of implemented subsystems and gives partial
credit to completed parts of a larger block. Implementation and qualification
have separate contributions.
The weights and contributions below are engineering estimates, not measured
work hours or a percentage of the acceptance ledger.
